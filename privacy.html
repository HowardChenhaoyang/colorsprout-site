ColorSprout privacy

Room to create.
Respect for privacy.

Effective date: October 27, 2026

ColorSprout does not require an account, upload your artwork to the developer, or include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Apple handles in-app payments. Contacting support and visiting this website involve separate data handling, explained below.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the ColorSprout iPhone and iPad app and this support website, maintained by the independent developer HowardChenhaoyang (referred to as “we” or “us”). You can contact us using the email at the end of this page.

Artwork and information on your device

The app stores your saved drawings and related information, such as the animal template and save time, locally in its app storage. We do not receive these files, and we do not operate a cloud artwork service. Apple device backups may include these files depending on your device and backup settings.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access. It does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Purchases handled by Apple

Optional purchases and restoration use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. The app reads verified transaction and entitlement information from Apple, including which product was purchased and whether access remains valid, to unlock content and respond to revocations. This information is not sent to a developer-operated server. We do not receive payment card details or your Apple Account password.

Apple processes purchase and account information under its own privacy policy. Apple may retain transaction records independently of whether you keep the app installed.

Children and families

Children can use the coloring features without providing personal information to us. Purchasing and restoring purchases are behind a parent check. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app or use behavioral advertising.

Support is intended for parents and guardians. Please do not send personal information about a child. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, such as your message, device details, or attachments. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot your request, and address privacy concerns. Our email service provider processes correspondence to deliver and store email.

We do not sell or rent your support information or use it for advertising. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations, then delete it. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your support correspondence using the contact email below. We may need to verify that you control the email address concerned.

This website

This website uses GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, contact forms, or third-party embedded content. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes when serving Pages websites. This hosting activity is separate from using the ColorSprout app. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its data handling and retention practices.

Storage, deletion, and your choices

Local artwork remains in the app’s storage until it is replaced or removed, or the app is deleted. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading it may retain that data. Manage device or iCloud backups through Apple’s settings. We cannot remotely access, recover, or delete drawings on your device or in your device backups.

There is no ColorSprout account to close. You can stop using the app at any time and contact us to request deletion of support messages. Requests concerning Apple’s transaction records or GitHub’s hosting logs should be directed to those providers.

Policy changes

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Material changes that require notice or consent will be addressed before the relevant new data handling begins.

Contact ColorSprout

Parents and guardians can email howardchenhaoyang@gmail.com for help or privacy questions. Support is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or a child’s personal information.
