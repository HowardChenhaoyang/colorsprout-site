Purchase and support information for parents and guardians using ColorSprout on iPhone or iPad.

Common questions

What is free, and what does the purchase unlock?

All ocean animals, ocean coloring, and the animated ocean scene are free. One non-consumable purchase permanently unlocks all current grassland and sky animals and their animated scenes. There is no subscription. The app displays the price provided by the App Store before you purchase. Future paid additions are not included in this promise.

How do I restore my purchase?
  1. Use the same Apple Account for App Store purchases that you used for the original purchase.
  2. Connect to the internet and open ColorSprout.
  3. Open the parent entry in the top-right corner of the animal gallery, or choose a locked grassland or sky category.
  4. Complete the parent check, then tap Restore purchases.

Restoring an eligible existing purchase does not charge you again. It restores access to content, not artwork from a previous installation. If nothing is found, check your App Store account and contact us.

A purchase is unavailable or waiting for approval.

Check your connection and App Store purchase restrictions, then try again from the parent area. A purchase awaiting payment or parental approval will unlock content after confirmation. You can continue using all ocean content while you wait.

Where are my pictures saved?

Completed artwork is saved in the app’s storage on your device. ColorSprout does not provide an account or its own cloud synchronization. Apple device backups may include app data depending on your settings.

Deleting the app removes its local artwork. Offloading the app is different and may retain app data. Restoring a purchase does not recover deleted drawings. Please contact us before reinstalling to troubleshoot a problem.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem website using the account that made the purchase. Apple decides refund eligibility. If Apple revokes a purchase, paid content may become locked again; saved artwork is not deleted by that change.

How do I report a problem?

Email us with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help, but remove personal information first. Do not include a child’s name, photograph, or other personal details.
